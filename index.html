01 / The case for economicsIndependent by design

Understand the harm. Assess the evidence.

A credible damages assessment explains how conduct affected the market, who may have suffered loss and how that loss can be assessed.

Competition disputes require a detailed understanding of pricing, costs, commercial relationships and market developments. The economic effects of an infringement may vary across products, customers and periods, and must be distinguished from other influences on performance.

We bring economic theory, empirical analysis and commercial evidence together to assess these effects and quantify any resulting loss. Our advice sets out the reasoning, alternative explanations and material uncertainties, giving counsel and businesses a sound basis for decisions throughout a matter.

03 / A considered position

Early assessment

A preliminary assessment considers the potential scale of harm, the available data and the principal analytical challenges. It helps clients judge what further work is proportionate and where it will add most value.

Evidence and strategy

Economics informs disclosure priorities, the scope of analysis and settlement considerations. We work with counsel to focus the investigation on the issues that matter at each stage.

Independent judgement

We advise claimants and defendants, applying the same standards of evidence and analysis. Our conclusions reflect the merits and limitations of the case, including findings that do not support an initial position.
